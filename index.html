About

Lim Yesung is a narrative engineer trained in film at Korea National University of Arts, working at the intersection of audiovisual storytelling and computer systems. She has worked as an assistant editor on the Netflix original series Move to Heaven and has experience in directing and broader screen production work. Beyond film, she has written game scenarios, developed, directed, and edited web drama projects, and authored a Grand Prize–winning web novel for Lezhin Comics. She is currently studying C and x86-64 assembly through Computer Systems: A Programmer’s Perspective (CSAPP), and explores film editing as a computational state space. Selected works can be found in the filmography, and the messier, code-driven half of her world lives on GitHub.
