About

Lim Yesung studied film at Korea National University of Arts. She has worked as an assistant editor on the Netflix original series Move to Heaven and has experience in directing and broader screen production work. Beyond film, she has written game scenarios, developed, directed, and edited web drama projects, and authored a Grand Prize–winning web novel for Lezhin Comics. She works through questions in film, mathematics, and computing on the blackboard. Selected works can be found in the filmography, and the messier, code-driven half of her world lives on GitHub.
