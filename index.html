About

Lim Yesung studied film at Korea National University of Arts. She worked as an assistant editor on the Netflix original series Move to Heaven, and co-directed and edited the web drama Find Me If You Can, which averaged 1.5 million views per episode. Outside film, she has written game scripts and a web novel, which won the Grand Prize in a Lezhin Comics contest and was then serialized there.

In 2026 she placed 6th as a solo entrant in the Inverse Galois Problem Competition (IGP24) and led a team that tied for 1st on the main leaderboard of the Mathematics Distillation Challenge: Equational Theories. Both were hosted by the Foundation for Science and AI Research (SAIR).

She works through questions in film, mathematics, and computing on the blackboard. Selected works can be found in the filmography, and the messier, code-driven half of her world lives on GitHub.
